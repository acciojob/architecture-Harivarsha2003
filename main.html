Objective

Functionality: Make sure the structure serves its clear, intended purpose and meets the daily needs of the people using it.Aesthetics and Harmony: Create visually pleasing forms and select colors, styles, and materials that fit well with the surrounding environment.Safety and Security: Protect occupants by following building codes and adding safety features like fire exits, strong structural frames, and security systems.Sustainability: Protect the earth by lowering energy use, using eco-friendly materials, and designing for energy efficiency.Cost-Effectiveness: Manage the budget carefully by choosing the right supplies and smart planning to reduce waste during construction.
